Services

Installation, turnkey erection and material-selection consulting for furnace, kiln and boiler linings.

What We Offer

Installation Services

Our team carries out installation of refractory linings for furnaces and boilers, working to client specification and industry-standard practice.

Turnkey Erection

From design through to commissioning, we offer turnkey erection services covering material supply, installation and handover for complete lining projects.

Consulting & Material Selection

Our team advises on refractory material selection for specific furnace, kiln and boiler conditions, helping clients choose the right grade and product for their application.
