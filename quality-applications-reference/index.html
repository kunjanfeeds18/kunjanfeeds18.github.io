Quality Assurance

Every batch of refractory material is tested in our laboratory against the relevant Indian Standard before dispatch. Tests performed include:

  • Cold Crushing Strength (CCS)
  • Apparent Porosity
  • Bulk Density
  • Pyrometric Cone Equivalent (PCE)
  • Refractoriness Under Load (RUL)
  • Permanent Linear Change (PLC)
  • Spalling Resistance
  • Thermal Conductivity
  • Chemical Analysis
  • Hot Modulus of Rupture
  • Sieve Analysis

Applications

Our refractory bricks and products are used in the construction and relining of furnaces across chemical, soda and silicate plants, oil refineries, power plants, and cement and steel plants, where consistent quality and dimensional accuracy are critical to lining life.

Beyond material supply, we undertake turnkey erection jobs for furnace and kiln linings, with our manufacturing process authenticated by the Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute (CGCRI) and built to ISI specification.

Industry / Furnace TypeTypical Operating Range
