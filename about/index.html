About J R Refractory

Manufacturing refractory bricks, castables and insulation products from Gujarat, India for over two decades.

From the Founder

Over 50 Years in Firebrick Manufacturing & Exports

For more than fifty years, I have been an entrepreneur, and for most of that journey my focus has been a single craft: the manufacture and export of firebricks. What began as a hands-on trade has grown into J R Refractory, built on a simple conviction that a refractory product is only as good as the furnace it protects.

From our works in Gujarat, India, we manufacture refractory bricks using a semi-dry process to ISI specification, with an annual capacity of around 10,000 metric tonnes. Over the decades, our range has widened to cover refractory castables and cements, insulating castables and bricks, acid proof bricks and mortars, ceramic fibre products, high temperature fabric, calcium silicate blocks, and clay and terracotta building products. These are supplied across India and exported to Saudi Arabia, the USA, Australia, the UK, Tanzania, Kuwait, Kenya, Myanmar, Sri Lanka and other countries. We also undertake turnkey erection work, and our manufacturing processes have been authenticated by the Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute (CGCRI). Through all of it, one thing has not changed: we stand behind every brick we ship. My team and I would be glad to help you choose the right material for your furnace, kiln or boiler.

— Founder & Managing Director, J R Refractory
