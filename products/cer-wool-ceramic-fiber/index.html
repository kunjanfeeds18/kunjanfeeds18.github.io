Cer-Wool Ceramic Fibre Products

Bulk fibre, blankets, modules, boards and speciality ceramic fibre products for furnace, kiln and industrial high-temperature insulation.

Bulk FibreBlanketsModulesBoards & ShapesSpecialities

Bulk Fibre

Loose ceramic fibre for packing irregular gaps, backup insulation and as a raw material for on-site fabrication, rated for continuous use up to 1260°C.

Ceramic Fibre Blankets

Ceramic Fibre Blankets
PropertyValue
Classification TemperatureUp to 1260°C
Density96–160 kg/m³

Ceramic Fibre Modules

Pre-compressed, folded blanket modules for rapid furnace wall and roof lining installation, available in anchored and veneered mounting formats.

Ceramic Fibre Boards & Shapes

Rigid ceramic fibre boards and vacuum-formed shapes for furnace linings, burner blocks and other components needing a self-supporting insulation form.
