Wire Cut & Exposed Bricks

Terracotta wire cut and exposed-face bricks, fired at 1100°C, for traditional, tropical and modern architectural brickwork.

Manufactured in Ahmedabad and fired at 1100°C, our wire cut and exposed bricks are suited to exposed side walls and feature brickwork across traditional, tropical and contemporary architectural styles.

  • Fired at 1100°C for consistent strength and colour
  • Brand JR, Terracotta red finish
  • Rectangular format, 9 × 4 × 3 in
  • Suitable for exposed side walls and decorative facades
