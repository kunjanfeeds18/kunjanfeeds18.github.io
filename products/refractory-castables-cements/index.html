Refractory Castables & Cements

Monolithic refractory castables, mortars and cements — JR Set 50, JR Crete Super, the JR Heat range and mortar cements — for furnace, kiln and boiler linings.

JR Set 50JR Crete SuperJR Heat RangeMortar CementsAlso Available

JR Set 50 (N)

A 50% alumina mortar (coarse grade) with a chemical/ceramic bond, suited to jointing and patching refractory brickwork.

JR Heat-A, Heat-K & Heat-C

Three high-alumina castable grades, each suited to a different service-severity zone.
