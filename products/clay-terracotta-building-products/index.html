Clay & Terracotta Building Products

Terracotta jali, wall tiles, face bricks and perforated bricks for architectural and decorative building use.

Alongside our refractory range, we supply a selection of clay and terracotta building products for architectural facades, feature walls and general masonry. Product photography for this range is being prepared — specifications below reflect material, finish and typical usage; contact us for samples and current availability.

Our Clay & Terracotta Range

Terracotta Jali

8 × 8 in perforated terracotta screen blocks, available in different designs, for ventilated facades, boundary walls and decorative partitions that need airflow with privacy.

  • Material: Terracotta clay
  • Size: 8 × 8 in
  • Designs: Multiple patterns available
  • Finish: Natural earthy red tones
  • Usage: Ventilated facades, screen walls, partitions

Terracotta Wall Tiles

Thin-profile terracotta tiles for exterior and interior wall cladding, available in brick-faced and shaded finishes.

  • Material: Terracotta clay
  • Format: Flat tile, brick-faced or shaded finish
  • Usage: Exterior and interior wall cladding

Clay Face Brick

Solid clay face bricks with a clean, consistent finish for exposed brickwork on building exteriors.

  • Material: Clay
  • Finish: Smooth, consistent face
  • Usage: Exposed exterior brickwork

Brick Tile

Slim brick-profile tiles that replicate the look of full brickwork at a fraction of the wall thickness, suited to retrofits and interior feature walls.

  • Material: Clay
  • Size: 9 × 3 in, 12 mm thick
  • Format: Slim tile, brick-face profile
  • Usage: Feature walls, retrofit cladding

Perforated Bricks

Bricks with regular perforations that reduce weight and material use while maintaining structural performance in general masonry walls.

  • Material: Clay
  • Format: Perforated, rectangular
  • Usage: General masonry walling
