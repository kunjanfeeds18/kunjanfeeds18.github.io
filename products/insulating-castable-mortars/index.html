Insulating Castable & Mortars

Medium iron insulating castables for furnace backup insulation, service temperatures up to 1350°C.

Medium Iron Insulating Castables

Our medium iron insulating castables are used as a lightweight backup layer behind dense refractory linings, reducing shell temperature and heat loss in furnaces, kilns and boilers.
