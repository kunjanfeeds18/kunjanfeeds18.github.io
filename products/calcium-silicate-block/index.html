Calcium Silicate Block

Lightweight calcium silicate insulation blocks, slabs, pipe sections and powder, rated for continuous service up to 1000°C.

Supplied as blocks, slabs, lags, pipe sections and powder, our calcium silicate insulation meets IS 8154 and IS 8428, ASTM C533-90 and BS 3958 Part II standards, for use wherever a rigid, lightweight, high-temperature insulation material is required.

Calcium Silicate Block — Grade Comparison

Linear Shrinkage< 2%< 2%

Advantages

  • Lightweight and easy to cut, drill and install on site
  • Low thermal conductivity reduces heat loss and surface temperature
  • Good compressive and flexural strength for a lightweight insulator
  • Dimensionally stable with low shrinkage at service temperature
  • Resistant to thermal shock and mechanical handling damage
  • Non-combustible and free of asbestos
