Refractory Bricks, Tiles & Blocks

High alumina fire clay bricks, tiles and blocks for kilns, chimneys and furnace linings, manufactured in JR30 to JR70 grades.

High Alumina Refractory Bricks

Manufactured from fire clay and bauxite for use in the construction of kilns, chimneys and furnace linings. Available in standard sizes 9 × 4.5 × 3 in, 9 × 6 × 3 in, 9 × 4.5 × 2.5 in and 9 × 4.5 × 1 in, with custom sizing on request.

High Alumina Refractory Tiles & Blocks

Supplied in the same JR30–JR70 alumina grades as our standard bricks (see table above), cut as tiles and blocks for lining applications where a lower-profile or larger-format unit is required — furnace hearths, kiln floors and chimney cladding.

Standard Shapes & Dimensions

Reference diagrams for our standard brick shapes and dimensional conventions, useful when specifying quantities and wall thicknesses for a lining job.

For Structural & Furnace Use

The same fire clay manufacturing base, supplied in grades suited to higher-duty industrial applications:

  • Cupola fire bricks for foundry cupola furnace linings
  • Fire bricks for steel industry furnaces and reheating applications
  • Clay bricks for tunnel kiln construction
  • High alumina fire bricks (standard and brown finish) for higher service-temperature zones

Also Available

Beyond our standard high alumina range, we also supply:

  • Fire clay, alumina and insulation-grade refractory bricks in brown, red and grey finishes
  • Refractory ceramic bricks and customised shaped bricks to drawing
  • Alumina, clay and silica refractory tiles
  • Refractory arch bricks for curved furnace sections
  • Bulk and granular refractory material (ceramic, sand, ash and crushed grades) for furnace bedding and boiler lining repair

Manufacturer and supplier of refractory bricks, castables, cements and insulation products, serving Indian industry for over two decades.
