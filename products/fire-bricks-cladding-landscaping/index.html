Fire Bricks for Cladding & Landscaping

Evenly finished fire bricks for decorative cladding, landscaping and interior feature walls.

For Cladding & Decorative Use

Evenly finished fire bricks with precise dimensional accuracy for exterior cladding, landscaping features, boundary walls and interior decorative brickwork.

  • Evenly finished face with consistent dimensional accuracy
  • Precise, repeatable design for clean wall and feature lines
  • Fire and heat resistant, suitable for exterior cladding
  • Rectangular format, 9 × 4.5 × 3 in (custom sizes available)
